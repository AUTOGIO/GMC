*Internal – Confidential. Single-Family Office Mandate.*

---

## GMC Mandate (One Line)

> **Giovannini Mare Capital exists to protect purchasing power first and to deploy capital aggressively only when payoff asymmetry is clearly in its favor.**

---

## Core Objectives (Strict Hierarchy)

1. **Avoid permanent capital loss**
2. **Preserve purchasing power (BRL-real terms)**
3. **Exploit macro regime transitions**
4. **Capture convex asymmetries**
5. **Compound capital selectively**

Return maximization is **secondary** to capital integrity.

---

## Investment Philosophy

The portfolio is constructed using a **risk-first, convex allocation framework** inspired by Convex Research.

**Core beliefs:**
- Markets are non-linear and regime-dependent
- Risk management precedes return optimization
- Survival through adverse regimes is a prerequisite for compounding
- Asymmetry (convexity) is superior to average optimization
- The portfolio is not designed to maximize returns in normal conditions, but to remain functional across extreme outcomes

---

## Risk Definition

Risk at GMC is **NOT** defined as volatility.

Risk is:
- **Permanent capital impairment**
- **Forced liquidation**
- **Loss of optionality during crises**

Drawdowns are acceptable. Fragility is not.

**Fragility assessment criteria:**
- Sensitivity to liquidity shocks
- Dependence on leverage
- Correlation shifts under stress
- Institutional positioning and forced-flow dynamics

Exposure is **scaled**, not binary.

---

## Portfolio Architecture — The Three Buckets ("Gavetas")

Capital is allocated across three functional buckets. Each bucket has a specific job.

### Bucket 1 — Survival & Optionality (35–55%)
**Job**: Survive crises, preserve liquidity, enable rebalancing.

Typical instruments:
- Cash / T-Bills / Tesouro Selic
- USD exposure (core hedge against BRL risk + rebalancing fuel)
- Gold (anti-fragile reserve)
- High-quality sovereign bonds (regime-dependent)

> This bucket will appear inefficient during calm markets. That is by design.

### Bucket 2 — Convex Growth (30–50%)
**Job**: Capture asymmetric upside in favorable regimes.

Typical instruments:
- Select equities (tech, secular growth, pricing power, balance-sheet strength)
- Cryptoassets: Bitcoin (regime-dependent reserve/convex) + selective alpha
- Commodities (inflation/geo shocks)
- Real assets (REITs) when convexity exists

Exposure expands **only** when fragility is low or improving.

### Bucket 3 — Tactical Brazil (0–15%)
**Job**: Opportunistic, non-structural positions.

- Brazil assets treated tactically only
- No permanent home-bias allocation
- Size limited to avoid portfolio contamination

## Barbell Logic

GMC follows a barbell strategy:

**Safety side:**
- Cash, USD assets, Gold, Sovereign bonds
- Goal: don't die; preserve purchasing power

**Optionality side:**
- Cryptoassets, select equities, commodities
- Goal: asymmetric upside

**Engine — disciplined rebalancing:**
- Crisis → rebalance from safety INTO optionality (buy cheap)
- Exuberance → trim optionality, rebuild safety

> The goal is structural convexity over time, not tactical brilliance.

---

## Rebalancing Rules

- Rebalancing is **event-driven**, not calendar-driven
- Inactivity is an **acceptable and often optimal** state
- Rebalancing occurs:
  - From safety → risk during crises (fragility ↓, assets cheap)
  - From risk → safety during exuberance (fragility ↑, assets expensive)

## Bitcoin Doctrine

Bitcoin's role in the portfolio is **state-dependent**:
- In fragility ↓ → behaves as convex optionality
- In fragility ↑ → behaves closer to reserve of value

**Key rule**: Bitcoin weight should never be justified by belief — only by payoff asymmetry.

---

## Real Estate Doctrine

From a Convex lens:
- Real estate is **not convex**
- It is **illiquid duration**
- Sensitive to: rates, credit availability, local economic cycles

Real estate belongs to the Capital Preservation / Structural layer but must not be treated as liquid ballast.

Implications:
- No aggressive expansion during tightening cycles
- Conservative valuation
- Used as long-term store, not rebalancing engine

## Convex Allocation — Summary Definition

> Convex allocation is building a portfolio whose payoff is asymmetric: structured to limit losses in stress regimes while preserving the ability to capture large upside in specific environments — via a barbell of safety + optionality and disciplined rebalancing.

This is **risk-structure design**, not asset hype.
