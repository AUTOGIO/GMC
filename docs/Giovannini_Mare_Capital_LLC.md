## 1. Purpose  
Giovannini Mare Capital LLC (“GMC”) exists to **preserve, protect, and compound family capital across generations**, with primary focus on **real purchasing power**, **capital resilience**, and **asymmetric opportunity capture** across macroeconomic regimes.  
## 2. Core Objectives (Hierarchy)  
1. **Avoid permanent capital loss**  
2. **Preserve purchasing power (BRL-real terms)**  
3. **Exploit macro regime transitions**  
4. **Capture convex asymmetries**  
5. **Compound capital selectively**  
Return maximization is **secondary** to capital integrity.  
## 3. Investment Style  
* Macro-driven, regime-aware  
* Concentrated when conviction is high  
* Defensive and liquid when uncertainty dominates  
* Cash treated as a **strategic asset**  
* No benchmark constraint  
## 4. Asset Universe  
* Cash & liquidity instruments  
* Global equities (selective, thematic)  
* Fixed income (duration as macro tool)  
* Commodities & real assets  
* FX and inflation hedges  
* Opportunistic alternatives  
Private assets are considered **only when asymmetry is clear**.  
## 5. Risk Philosophy  
* Risk is defined as **irreversible loss**, not volatility  
* Drawdowns are acceptable; fragility is not  
* Liquidity is mandatory in late-cycle regimes  
* Exposure is reduced aggressively when conditions deteriorate  
## 6. Governance  
* Single decision authority (CIO / Principal)  
* No delegation without conviction alignment  
* Independent thinking prioritized over consensus
